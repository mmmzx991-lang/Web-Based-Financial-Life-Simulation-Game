# Market refinement and simulation depth

Authorized changes: remove developer/prototype commentary from both player languages, refine market navigation/details, allow crypto orders in0.01 increments, deepen simulation without adding real market feeds or breaking proposal settlement rules.

Interfaces: new games have marketVersion3 and scenario seed standard-v3; unversioned old saves retain the legacy v2 market path. Fair leaderboard categories default to current marketVersion3, excluding legacy runs from the new table. Saves are never deleted. All Standard paths remain independent of player decisions. New macro state is `game.macro` with annual policyRate,inflation,growth, confidence/creditStress/liquidity in0..1, monthly propertyReturn, rentDemand,businessDemand,inputCostGrowth. `game.assetMetrics[id]` may expose estimated volatility/valuation/trend; `game.priceHistory[id]` holds monthly price observations including initial. Existing history snapshots stay append-only.

- Crypto precision: validate0.01 steps using integer hundredths, canonicalize quantity arithmetic, round all cash/exposure/portfolio values to integer sen, preserve locks, average cost and position margin cap. Noncrypto remains integer units. Public asset config quantityStep=.01 forcrypto,1 otherwise.
- Market model: seeded variable-duration economic regime, persistent macro/rate/inflation factors, correlated category shocks, asset-specific fundamentals/mean reversion and volatility clustering, news persistence without duplicate monthly effects. Property and business respond sensibly to macro conditions. Bound parameters to keep a696-month educational scenario usable. Fixed salary/expenses/opening rules unchanged.
- UI: group assets by market/category/subcategory, search/sort, risk/unit/quantity info, price chart on instrument detail. Keep both languages complete. Remove template counts, no-live-feed notes, README/config/proposal/implementation labels from all product screens; keep actual game rules available in help.
- Verification: precision/accounting, non-fixed regime, deterministic/replay invariants, macro sensitivity, full-mode runs, UI clean-copy checks, browser0.01crypto roundtrip and categorized market navigation. Independent review then direct push and local service refresh.
