# Simulation implementation notes (save v2 / market v3)

New games use the v3 market model described in [MARKET-MODEL.md](MARKET-MODEL.md); old saves retain legacy v2 behavior. Crypto quantities use exact hundredths, with each holding/position rounded separately at the integer-sen accounting boundary.

Money is integer sen. Every exported transition clones its input. `history` is append-only: the first snapshot is elapsed month zero, followed by exactly one closing snapshot for each completed month. `report().history` is the same history array. Current actions may change the live balance between closing snapshots; charts use the saved snapshots.

## Public data and actions

The exports and action names follow `CONTRACT.md`. Configuration names are `{en,zh}`. Difficulties contain `cash22`, `salary22`, `cash45`, `cash60`, `properties45`, `properties60`, and Expert's `mortgage60`. Property types contain `value`, `rent`, `maintenance`, `downPayment`, `annualRate`, and `term`. Business types contain `cost`, `baseValue`, `revenue`, `operatingCost`, `advertisingCost`, and `upgradeCost`.

Reports additionally expose `savings` and `retirementBalance`. `score` components and total retain full precision; presentation may round them. `drawdown` is a fraction (0.25 means 25%). The optional age-45 milestone becomes true if saved real net worth reaches RM1 million at the 45th birthday. It does not block completion.

`SETTINGS` in `server/core/config.js` makes parameters absent from the proposal explicit:

- Trade fee: 0.2%, rounded to sen with a minimum of one sen, applied once per buy or sale. A leveraged entry pays its fee on exposure; close fees cannot exceed remaining position equity.
- Savings interest: 3% annual, paid monthly into savings. Retirement account: 4% annual, paid monthly into the account. Deposits and withdrawals transfer assets; retirement withdrawals begin at 60.
- Personal loans: 7.5% annual, RM200,000 maximum outstanding personal principal, terms of 12/24/36/60 months. Mortgages: 20% down payment, 4.5%, 240 months. No loan origination or early repayment fee. Mortgage payments and fixed-rule personal payments use outstanding principal/rate/remaining term; rounding is corrected by the final payment.
- Legacy property values appreciate 2% annual in monthly increments; v3 values follow macro growth, rates, credit and common property shocks. Property sales cost 2%. New properties start vacant; opening properties start occupied. Default tenant matching chance is 65% per eligible month. Matching in month M starts rent in M+1. Legacy vacancy events can occur every 43 months; v3 vacancies and tenant matching depend on rental demand, with annual rent review and inflation-linked maintenance. Individual property `tenantSearchProbability` is an optional internal test override, not a public action.
- Business setup converts player cash into base business value. Revenue varies with the economy and a deterministic business-specific stream; legacy operating costs are fixed by type and active level. V3 revenues and costs retain separate shocks and inherit inflation, with type-specific demand/credit sensitivity. Each business exposes `lastRevenue`, `lastOperatingCost`, and `lastProfit` from its most recent monthly operation (zero before its first operation); these are actual results rather than configuration estimates. Retained profit remains business cash and is included once in `business.value = baseValue + cash`. Dividends transfer that cash to the player. Advertisements cost cash and lift revenue for the following three months (legacy 16%; v3 diminishing returns from audience saturation). Upgrades convert player cash into base value, become productive from the next settlement and support five levels. Sales cost 3% and settle before that month's operations. An operating loss first consumes retained cash; any remaining deficit joins the player's bill.
- Long-only leverage: integer 1/2 for stocks, gold and commodities, 1/2/3 for crypto. Leveraged positions carry margin equity as the asset, with no separate exposure asset or margin loan. Position equity is `max(0, margin + quantity * (currentPrice-entryPrice))`. An equity value at or below 15% of original margin triggers liquidation at the monthly update; losses cannot exceed committed margin, apart from the disclosed entry fee. New positions remain voluntarily locked until the next market update.
- Extension fee: RM50, once per unpaid bill. No extension crosses the mode's ending birthday.

## Monthly order and interrupted advancement

The common monthly procedure settles due property/business sales, updates the public market and price index once, unlocks holdings, checks leveraged risk, applies career/tenant/business outcomes, credits ownership-based income, creates the month's single bill, and closes the month if payment succeeds. The closing age then increments; salary grows on a working birthday and becomes zero at 60. No opening assets are awarded when Full Life crosses a life stage.

`marketMonth` counts applied market updates. `elapsedMonths` counts finalized closing records. A partially processed month can therefore have `marketMonth = elapsedMonths + 1` while its unpaid bill is being resolved. Cash, salary, markets, interest, loan amortization, business results and news have already been applied to that pending month and must not be replayed.

`pendingSettlement` contains `{month, ledger}`. The ledger records salary, bonus, rent, dividends, savings/retirement interest, business revenue/costs, loan principal/interest, fees, income, expenses and event outcomes. It also has a unique `billId`, this month's `billTotal`, `billPaid` and `carriedUnpaid`. Principal is a transfer and is excluded from expenses. Asset sale proceeds and dividends from retained business cash are transfers rather than newly earned income.

An invoice first uses available cash. `unpaidBill` is either null or `{id,amount,createdMonth,extended,dueMonth}`; `amount` is the remaining liability. Loan principal due is removed from its original loan when invoiced and becomes part of that bill, so it is never counted twice. Loan interest remains a real expense. A month with an unpaid amount increments `unpaidMonths` once, and any due loan payments increment `latePayments` once.

- `pay-bill` pays the remaining amount and finalizes the original pending month without replaying it. If fast-forward months remain, status stays paused and `resume` continues them. `resume` can also pay a funded pending bill directly.
- `extend-bill` adds the fee, retains the same unpaid bill, finalizes the pending month, and pauses for player decisions. It preserves remaining advance months and guarantees one subsequent settlement. A property/business listed while paused cannot settle before this next settlement. If proceeds and new income still cannot pay all carried/current bills then, that month closes with bankruptcy and one default. Additional extensions are rejected.
- `declare-bankruptcy` requires both an unpaid bill and an unfinalized `pendingSettlement`. It closes that pending failure month and produces a bankruptcy report. The UI should show manual bankruptcy only when `pendingSettlement` exists. After an extension has finalized the original month, the player must `resume` the following due settlement; it automatically ends in bankruptcy if funds are still insufficient. This ensures actions taken after the extension enter the next closing ledger and final report, while the previously saved snapshot remains unchanged for charts and request replay.
- Required career reviews occur in working month 6 and each 60 months thereafter. The 3% raise is applied once for the following month before the review is shown. `pendingEvent` includes bilingual title/body, `id`, `type`, `effectApplied` and `salaryMultiplier`. `acknowledge-event` clears it and automatically continues remaining advance months when no bill blocks progress. Separate half-salary career bonuses occur in working months 18, 42, etc. Retired players have no career events.

All action validation errors throw. A paused run permits selling unlocked assets, funding transfers, taking/replaying loans, and listing disposals; new trades/property/business purchases require resolving the pause. Request IDs and durable request-response deduplication belong to the HTTP repository layer.

## Scenario, benchmark and scoring

Standard scenario seed depends only on mode and difficulty. Public prices, economy, news and inflation use stateless monthly streams; player actions never consume their randomness. Free games can accept an internal numeric seed, otherwise they receive a time-derived seed. Legacy v2 rotates 60 authored bilingual templates, uses four fixed 18-month phases and monthly inflation around 0.2%. V3 uses seeded non-repeating selection, news impulses spread across three months, variable-duration regimes and persistent macro inflation/rates/credit/demand. Fixed stage living budgets do not inflate in either version.

The conservative benchmark begins with identical opening cash, properties and loans. Positive benchmark cash automatically earns the savings rate. It receives the same scheduled salary/career rules, fixed stage living budgets, reference rents, maintenance and amortized opening mortgages. Opening properties stay occupied for the conservative comparison and receive the same macro property return as the player (legacy fixed configured rate). It makes no new trades or purchases. A negative benchmark cash balance remains in its benchmark balance rather than ending the comparison.

Real balances are nominal balances divided by the price index, rounded to sen. Final wealth score uses the proposal's RM10,000 stabilizer (1,000,000 sen). Risk uses the maximum nominal peak-to-trough drawdown from saved history plus forced liquidations. Cash flow uses months with unpaid bills; debt uses late payments and defaults. Components clamp to 0–100 and total weights are 60% / 15% / 15% / 10%. The bankruptcy and completed flags are mutually exclusive.

## Leverage refinement

Browser and server use the pure `public/leverage-math.js` close quote. Partial closing allocates integer margin and equity proportionally; the remaining position carries a rounding adjustment so allocated equity plus residual equity exactly matches the original. Closing in parts cannot create sen. The liquidation estimate binary-searches the highest integer-sen price meeting the existing 15% equity/margin rule and is not a guaranteed execution price.

New positions (`positionVersion:2`) pay the same 0.2% exposure-based exit fee on manual and forced closure, capped at available position equity. Existing positions without this flag retain their legacy forced fee on equity. Entry fee remains separately expensed. History reports P&L after exit fee but excludes the already-paid entry fee; financial reports retain both. No funding interest or shorts are added.
